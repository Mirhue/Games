# Kingpin v4 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Seven gangs with their own raid effects, gangs that grow, heal and retaliate, street encounters, and 24 small skills including a Warfare branch.

**Architecture:** Same layers. The new rules live in two new pure modules, re-exported from `engine.js`:
- `js/gangs.js`: raid effects, growth, healing, retaliation.
- `js/encounters.js`: rolling and resolving encounters.

All numbers are in `data.js`. The sim drives the real engine.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla ES modules; Node 22 tests; Playwright for browser checks.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-kingpin-v4-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- All v1–v3 constraints hold: no deps, pure engine, integer cash, tunables in `data.js`.
- The vault balance and metals are never taken by any raid.
- Every new perk goes through `perk()` with a cap in `PERK_CAPS` (except powerPct).

## Review Focus
1. **A pending encounter across reload / offline:** jobs are blocked, the encounter survives migrate, and garbage is dropped. Tested.
2. **Raid effects with nothing to hit:** no weapons, no business or an empty vault fall back to cash; nothing goes negative. Tested.
3. **Retaliation and offline:** an offline raid uses `raidFrom`, and the forced raider is cleared after use. Tested.
4. **The Commission:** it can't be attacked, and doesn't raid, before the other six are defeated. Tested.
5. **Skill chain with inserted minors:** a v3 save owning `stealth2` can still upgrade it, and `stealth1m` is buyable. Tested.
